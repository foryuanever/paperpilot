# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chinese university students, tutors, research teams, and platform administrators who manage papers, AI-assisted reading, academic collaboration, team work, and platform operations.

## Product Purpose

PaperSlover is an integrated academic workspace. It helps users collect and search literature, read and analyze papers with AI, prepare group-meeting reports, collaborate in teams, communicate in the research forum, and manage usage quotas. Administrators operate shared AI routes, user accounts, teams, billing, and site-wide messages.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear, efficient. The interface should feel trustworthy for sustained academic work while remaining approachable to students.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative glass effects, cramped typography, oversized empty areas, browser-native alerts, ambiguous controls, and configuration options shown to users who lack permission.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current academic task before decoration.
2. Make permissions and system status explicit.
3. Keep dense research information readable and scannable.
4. Use one consistent interaction vocabulary across user and admin surfaces.
5. Preserve user work across refreshes and navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear Chinese labels, keyboard-visible focus states, sufficient text contrast, non-color-only status indicators, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
